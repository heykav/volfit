volfit live implied-vol surface, fitted and checked in your browser

Pulls the public Deribit BTC or ETH option book, inverts a Black-76 implied vol from each out-of-the-money bid/ask mid, fits a raw SVI smile per expiry, and tests every fit for butterfly and calendar arbitrage. No server, no API key. Source

  • Data is Deribit's public crypto option book, fetched when you load or refresh the page. It is a snapshot of quotes, not a trade record, and can be stale or thin in the wings.
  • Implied vol uses the bid/ask mid of out-of-the-money options only, priced with Black-76 on the forward Deribit reports per expiry (no interest-rate input). Quotes with no two-sided market are dropped.
  • The SVI fit is least squares in total variance with bounds on the kink location, width and wing slope. It is not a global no-arbitrage fit: the checks report what holds on the fitted curve over the quoted strike range, and a pass says nothing about strikes outside it.
  • Calendar checks compare adjacent fitted expiries only where both have quotes.
  • This is a modelling and visualisation tool, not trading advice, and nothing here is a price you can trade at.
